01 / About the creative

About
Creative.

Genius Mambwe is a Creative Lead and Head of MagiqWorqs, a premium branding and marketing agency focused on high-impact brand experiences.

Born on 27th February 1997 in Kitwe, Zambia, he works with ambitious, high-end brands, elevating them through strategic creative direction, branding, PR, and marketing.

At MagiqWorqs, he develops brand identities and campaigns that not only look exceptional but deliver real results, turning brands into trusted, memorable experiences.
